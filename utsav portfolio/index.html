// questions

About the 24 hours

How can a website be ready in 24 hours?

I work with a tested process and modern tools, and you deal with one person instead of a team. There are no meetings to schedule and no handoffs, so the work moves quickly.

When does the 24 hours start?

When I have your brief and your content: the text for each page, your logo and any photos. If you don't have text yet, I can write it for you, which may add a little time.

What if I want changes after launch?

Small fixes in the first week are free. After that, changes are covered by the care plan, or I'll quote them separately.

Do I need my own domain and hosting?

If you already have a domain, I'll connect it. If not, I'll help you buy one and set up hosting, which is often free for small sites.
